# Retrieval‑Augmented Generation in Customer Support: Uses, Benefits, and Limitations

Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) blends large language models (LLMs) with a real‑time retrieval layer that pulls up‑to‑date documents or knowledge‑base passages. In customer‑support scenarios, RAG can answer queries faster, more accurately, and with built‑in provenance. This article summarizes the most relevant, evidence‑backed findings and caveats for deploying RAG in enterprise support environments.

---

## 1. What Is RAG?

RAG adds a retrieval step before the LLM generates a response. The LLM receives a query *and* the most relevant snippets from an external document store (e.g., CRM, billing, knowledge‑base). The model then composes a grounded answer that references those snippets. This architecture keeps the LLM lightweight while allowing it to access dynamic, domain‑specific knowledge without retraining the model.

| **Live chat assistance** | Offers agents live citations from product documentation or internal knowledge‑bases, reducing answer time. |
| **Claims processing** | Uses current claim logs and policy clauses to guide agents or bots through claim workflows. |
